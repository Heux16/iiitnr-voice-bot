## Medical Center, Dispensary & 24x7 Ambulance

- **On-Campus Health Center**: IIIT-NR maintains an on-campus Medical Dispensary / Health Center staffed by a resident Medical Officer (Doctor), nurse, and emergency medical attendant.
- **Services Provided**: First aid, routine outpatient consultations, basic diagnostic tests, vitals monitoring, and dispensing of essential prescription drugs.
- **24x7 Emergency Ambulance**: A dedicated, fully equipped emergency ambulance is stationed on campus 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for rapid emergency transport of students or staff to premier hospitals in Raipur (including AIIMS Raipur and Balaji Hospital).
- **Emergency Medical Helpline**: Emergency calls can be placed directly to the security main gate and campus dispensary around the clock.

## Hostel Living & Night Gate Regulations

- **Compulsory Residential Campus**: IIIT-NR is a 100% residential campus with separate multi-story hostel buildings for male and female students equipped with high-speed Wi-Fi, study rooms, gymnasiums, and dining halls.
- **Hostel Curfew Timings**: The mandatory night curfew time for students to return to campus and enter the hostels is **10:00 PM**.
- **Night Gate Pass & Leave**: Students leaving campus overnight or for weekend home visits must submit an approved **Night Gate Pass** authenticated by the Hostel Warden / Chief Warden prior to departure. Unapproved late entry is strictly subject to disciplinary review.
